# Content standards

The English expansion adds three unapproved teaching drafts and 15 unapproved sample questions, bringing the public lesson catalog to eight and the two editorial packs to 35 questions. The one-question demo seed remains unchanged. See ENGLISH_DRAFTS.md; automated checks are not independent review.

All original educational material begins as DRAFT and is a clearly labeled editorial preview, unavailable to paid exams/PDF publication. Require a separate qualified human review before REVIEWED; publication requires approval and an immutable version. Store author/reviewer identity, time, source notes, level, topic, difficulty, explanation and version. AI generation alone is not review.

Each assessment question has four distinct options and exactly one defensible correct answer. Validate duplicate normalized prompts, option identifiers, missing explanations/source notes, incomplete metadata and version/review status. An automated check cannot certify factual accuracy or originality; independent checking remains required. Use UTF-8 English and Filipino.

Lessons need learning objectives, explanation, worked example, practice and rationale. The foundation includes one complete draft arithmetic sample and a 25-chapter provisional outline; the outline is not a finished e-book. Never import commercial reviewer material, actual confidential exam questions, CSC logos or passing guarantees.

Phase 5E expands the public previews to five numerical lessons and adds a 20-question DRAFT review pack alongside the unchanged one-question seed. Automated arithmetic/schema checks do not count as independent review. Public teaching solutions are separate from server-owned graded attempt snapshots; the public-source pack is not a confidential bank. Read DRAFT_LESSONS.md before import or release.

Before launch: 500+ independently checked original questions; proofread all lessons and Filipino text; legal and historical claims checked against authoritative text; verify 2027 coverage; confirm accessibility and PDF output. Never infer official dates, time limits, weights or scoring from previous years.

The Phase 2 application uses DRAFT → IN_REVIEW → APPROVED → PUBLISHED, with rejection returning a submitted version to DRAFT and archival retaining its audit trail. APPROVED is the database representation of an independently reviewed version. Authors cannot approve their own versions; approval records bind the exact content hash, and published versions cannot be edited. A later revision is a new draft version with no inherited approval.

Draft PDFs are private staff proofs. A reviewed free PDF preview has an additional whole-document human review/release step for the generated template and notices. The FULL edition gate requires 500 unique published, independently approved question keys. Simulated automated-test approvals occur only in a disposable database and do not satisfy any human/content launch requirement.
